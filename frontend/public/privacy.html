Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 9, 2026

Welcome to ROXY Personal AI ("ROXY", "we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you access and use our application at https://roxy-personal-ai.vercel.app.

1. Information We Collect

When you use ROXY Personal AI, we may collect the following information:

  • Account & Authentication Information: When you sign in using Google OAuth or GitHub OAuth, we collect your primary email address, full name, and avatar/profile picture as provided by the OAuth provider.
  • Usage Data & Interactions: We process your text prompts, voice inputs, and uploaded documents strictly to generate responses and execute requested assistant actions (such as calculations, notes, calendar assistance, and workflows).
  • Session Data: We generate and maintain secure session tokens (JWT) to keep you signed in across browser sessions.

2. How We Use Google OAuth Data

Our use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • We request only basic authentication scopes (openid, email, profile) to securely verify your identity and create your user account.
  • We do not sell your Google user data to any third party or data broker.
  • We do not use your personal Google account data for targeted advertising or marketing purposes.
  • We do not transfer your Google account data to third parties, except as required to fulfill core functionality (e.g., executing authorized user requests through our AI backend) or to comply with applicable laws.

3. Data Storage & Security

We implement industry-standard encryption, token verification (RS256/HMAC), and secure cookie standards (SameSite, HttpOnly, and Secure flags in production) to protect your authentication tokens and user records.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your account data and session records only as long as necessary to provide the ROXY service. You have the right to request access to your data or request complete deletion of your account and associated records at any time.

To request deletion of your account or data, please contact our support team at the email address listed below.

5. Third-Party Services

ROXY integrates with third-party authentication and cloud hosting providers, including Google Identity Services, GitHub OAuth, and Vercel. Each provider operates under its respective privacy terms.
